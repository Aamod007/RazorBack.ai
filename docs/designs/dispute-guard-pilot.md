## Problem Statement
Mid-market D2C merchants selling in India through Razorpay face a constant stream of payment disputes and chargebacks. Each dispute comes with a strict `respond_by` countdown (typically 3–7 business days). Missing this SLA results in an automatic loss of both the order funds and the merchandise, accompanied by gateway dispute penalty fees (₹500–₹1,500). Today, merchant operations teams either surrender and auto-accept every dispute (forfeiting winnable revenue) or waste 20–45 minutes per dispute manually hunting down tracking IDs, courier proofs of delivery (PODs), customer invoices, and support chat transcripts across disparate dashboards.

## Demand Evidence
Direct conversations with fast-scaling D2C merchants operating at 500+ orders/day confirmed that chargebacks and friendly fraud ("item not received" despite delivery) directly erode net margins. Ops leads report that handling surges during festival sales or flash promotions results in missed response deadlines and thousands of rupees in preventable financial leakage.

## Status Quo
Manual multi-dashboard scramble: When a `payment.dispute.created` notification arrives, an ops or finance analyst logs into Razorpay, copies the payment and customer identifiers, switches tabs to Shopify or WooCommerce to fetch order line items and customer emails, switches to Shiprocket or Delhivery to locate the AWB and download the delivery confirmation PDF, manually re-uploads each document into the matching Razorpay Documents API categories, drafts a summary letter, and submits before the deadline.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Target User:** Ops / Finance Lead at a scaling Indian D2C brand processing 500+ orders/day. They are directly measured on SLA compliance, dispute win rate, and operational overhead.
- **Narrowest Wedge:** A standalone "Co-Pilot & Connector" that integrates directly with the merchant's Razorpay test/live API keys. It automatically detects disputes via webhooks, scores win probability using calibrated XGBoost, auto-populates all available documents from order/courier records, drafts the contest payload in Razorpay, and gives the analyst a 1-click "Approve & Submit" queue with deadline timers—with automatic auto-contest only for verified deliveries under a safe dollar ceiling (₹2,000).

## Constraints
- **Razorpay API Contract:** Evidence must map strictly to Razorpay's recognized slots (`shipping_proof`, `billing_proof`, `customer_communication`, `proof_of_service`, etc.) and documents must be uploaded to `POST /v1/documents` before referencing them in `PATCH /v1/disputes/:id/contest`.
- **Zero Fabrication Hard Rule:** If a required document (e.g. signed POD) does not exist in courier records, the system must leave that slot empty and degrade the completeness score—never invent or synthesize documents.
- **SLA & Rate Limits:** Dispute submissions must conclude ahead of `respond_by`. Failed uploads must retry with exponential backoff (max 3 tries) and escalate gracefully to human review rather than silently dropping or auto-accepting.
- **Single-Tenant Pilot Simplicity:** Deployable as a single-tenant container or lightweight service to facilitate immediate piloting with 1–3 design partner brands without upfront multi-tenant SaaS overhead.

## Premises
1. **Orchestration over Hallucination:** Value lies in rapid multi-system data stitching (Razorpay + Shopify + logistics tracking) and strict API compliance, not creative AI writing.
2. **Calibrated Gating over Blind Fighting:** Machine learning scores win odds honestly. Low-value unwinnable disputes (<₹500) are auto-accepted to preserve bandwidth, while high-probability cases are contested with complete evidence packets.
3. **Gradual Autonomy over Black-Box Control:** Financial actions are bounded by configurable money ceilings, an immutable audit log, and an escalation queue for high-value or edge-case disputes.

## Approaches Considered
- **Approach A: Standalone Co-Pilot & Direct Merchant Connector (Selected)**
  - Fast-to-pilot architecture built on the existing FastAPI + SQLite/Postgres + XGBoost engine. Provides 1-click review queue and instant live key connectors.
- **Approach B: Multi-Tenant Cloud Native Risk Engine (Ruled Out for Phase 1)**
  - Full multi-tenant SaaS with Celery/Redis, OAuth2 app store distribution, and tenant partitioning. Ruled out to avoid delaying live merchant validation with excessive cloud plumbing.
- **Approach C: Upstream Dispute Preemption & Customer Concierge (Ruled Out for Phase 1)**
  - Automated WhatsApp messaging to resolve customer grievances before chargeback finalization. Ruled out as v2 scope to keep pilot focus on winning active disputes.

## Recommended Approach
Approach A: Standalone Co-Pilot & Direct Merchant Connector.
It leverages 100% of the verified backend codebase (14 passing unit tests covering decision gate, evidence agent, failure handling, risk scorer, and webhook HMAC verification) and the working ops dashboard. It provides the fastest path to validating actual willingness to pay and real win-rate lift with live merchant partners.

## Success Criteria
- **Win-Rate Lift:** Contested dispute win rate exceeds the merchant's historical baseline by at least 25%.
- **Zero Missed Deadlines:** 100% of disputes resolved or drafted before the `respond_by` timestamp.
- **Ops Time Reduction:** Dispute processing time drops from 30+ minutes to under 60 seconds per dispute.
- **Audit Integrity:** 100% of decisions recorded with immutable timestamps, model scores, and rule justifications.

## Engineering Architecture Specifications (Locked via /plan-eng-review)

### 1. Decision Gate Boundary Locking
- **Amount Ceiling Inversion:** The Amount Ceiling (`AMOUNT_CEILING_INR = 2000.0`) is evaluated **strictly before** the Deadline Failsafe. High-value disputes (`amount > ₹2,000`) always route to the human review queue as `CONTEST_DRAFT` (or urgent escalation if approaching SLA), and can never be autonomously submitted.
- **Rule Hierarchy State Diagram:**
```
[Inbound Dispute]
       │
       ▼
[Rule 0: Upload Failure?] ── Yes ──► [ESCALATE (Never silent accept)]
       │ No
       ▼
[Rule 1: Amount > ₹2,000?] ── Yes ──► [CONTEST_DRAFT (Human Queue)]
       │ No (<= ₹2,000)
       ▼
[Rule 2: Hours <= 12h & Comp >= 0.40?] ── Yes ──► [CONTEST_AUTO (Deadline Failsafe)]
       │ No
       ▼
[Rule 3: Amount <= ₹500 & WinProb <= 0.25?] ── Yes ──► [ACCEPT (Auto-concede)]
       │ No
       ▼
[Rule 4: WinProb >= 0.65 & Comp >= 0.70 & No Missing?] ── Yes ──► [CONTEST_AUTO]
       │ No
       ▼
[Rule 5: Default] ──► [CONTEST_DRAFT (Human Queue)]
```

### 2. Razorpay Documents API Schema Compliance
- `EvidencePacket` stores `uploaded_slot_docs: Dict[str, List[str]]` mapping document IDs back to official reason-code categories (`shipping_proof`, `billing_proof`, `customer_communication`, etc.).
- `PATCH /v1/disputes/:id/contest` submits valid categorized keys rather than flat `doc_N` arrays.

### 3. Data Model Integrity & Concurrency
- SQLite initialized with `PRAGMA journal_mode = WAL;` and `PRAGMA busy_timeout = 30000;` to prevent database locks during concurrent webhook ingestion.
- Added `GET /api/disputes/{id}/replay` and `replay_dispute_events()` to provide verifiable chronological state machine replay across audit events.

### 4. Expanded Test Suite
- Comprehensive end-to-end integration tests (`tests/test_e2e_pipeline.py`) covering webhook HMAC verification, XGBoost scoring, document upload, decision execution, and audit log generation.
- Boundary and failure tests (`tests/test_boundary_failures.py`) asserting:
  - Dispute > ₹2,000 with <12h remaining is held for human review (never auto-contested).
  - Razorpay contest API 5xx failure routes to graceful failure escalation.
  - Duplicate webhook receipt triggers idempotent handling without double document upload.
  - Audit replay verification test.

## Design Review Specifications (Locked via /plan-design-review)

### 1. Information Architecture & 3-Second Scan (10/10)
- **Executive Verdict Banner:** Sticky modal header displaying Dispute Status, Amount in INR, Decision Rule, and XGBoost Win Probability bar at the top of the detail modal.
- **3-Tier Hierarchy:** Primary (Verdict & Win Probability) -> Secondary (Evidence Checklist with green/amber indicators) -> Tertiary (Technical audit logs & raw JSON nested in collapsible accordions).

- **Guided Empty State:** Zero-data dashboard replaces blank table space with a branded protective shield, explanatory copy, and a 1-click CTA button to seed an archetypal test dispute.
- **Shimmer Skeletons:** Table data fetches render smooth CSS pulse skeletons to eliminate layout shifts during refresh intervals.

### 3. Emotional Arc & SLA Countdown Urgency (10/10)
- **Dynamic SLA Heartbeat:** Color-coded countdown badges (`> 24h` calm slate, `12h - 24h` alert amber, `< 12h` urgent pulsing crimson) conveying visceral situational urgency.
- **Margin Relief Badges:** Celebrates recovered capital with explicit 'Contested & Protected' states and instant link to the cryptographic Evidence Binder.

### 4. Design System & Visual Polish (10/10)
- **Luminous Button Hierarchy:** Electric blue primary button glow, warning amber for concession, and outline style for secondary exports.
- **Iconized Status Pills:** Colorblind-safe status pills prefixed with icons (`⚡ Auto-Contested`, `🔒 Draft Pending`, `❌ Conceded`).
- **Custom Scrollbars:** Styled slim scrollbars matching the dark-mode aesthetic.

### 5. AI Slop Detection & Removal (10/10)
- **Enterprise Treasury Language:** Replaced hackathon meta-commentary ("Track Bar Demands") with authentic institutional risk terminology: "P&L Impact & False-Positive Cost Analysis".
- **Net Capital Protected Callout:** Direct calculation of recovered capital minus operational costs.

### 6. Responsive Viewport Ergonomics (10/10)
- **Mobile Card Transformation:** Below 640px, wide tables reflow into touch-friendly cards with guaranteed 48px touch targets for thumb navigation.
- **Mobile Bottom-Sheet:** Detail modal slides up from screen bottom on mobile viewports.

### 7. Accessibility & Edge Case Hardening (10/10)
- **Keyboard Ergonomics:** `Escape` key immediately closes the active modal; `:focus-visible` rings provide accessible keyboard focus navigation.
- **ARIA Standards:** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` semantics.
- **Truncation Guards:** CSS `text-overflow: ellipsis` protects against 50-character customer names and extreme INR currency amounts.
